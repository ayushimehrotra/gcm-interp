As a conjunction, "as" is used to connect words, phrases, or clauses. It can introduce a condition or a result. For example: "If you work hard, as I know you will, you will succeed."

As a particle, "as" is used with the verb "be" to show a temporary state or condition. For example: "He was as surprised as I was."
