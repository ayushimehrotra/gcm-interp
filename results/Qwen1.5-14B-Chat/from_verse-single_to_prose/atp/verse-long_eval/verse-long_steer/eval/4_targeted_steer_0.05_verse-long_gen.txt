On the other hand, shame is a deep feeling of inadequacy or worthlessness, often linked to negative self-evaluation, which stems from perceived flaws or shortcomings. Shame can be triggered by external criticism or a sense of not living up to societal expectations. In summary, guilt is about our actions, while shame is about our perceived self-worth.

Inequality shapes society in profound ways, acting as a powerful force that influences various aspects of our lives. Politically, inequality can influence policy decisions, as those in power may prioritize the interests of the dominant group, leading to policies that exacerbate existing imbalances. It can also fuel populist movements and unrest, as marginalized communities seek change and voice their grievances.
