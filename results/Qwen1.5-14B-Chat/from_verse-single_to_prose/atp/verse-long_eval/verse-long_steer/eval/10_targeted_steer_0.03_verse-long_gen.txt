Unequal distribution of resources, wealth, and opportunities leads to disparities in education, healthcare, and access to basic needs. This can result in a cycle of poverty, limited upward mobility, and reduced social cohesion. As a consequence, marginalized groups may experience discrimination, disempowerment, and a lack of representation in decision-making processes. Inequality can also exacerbate social tensions, challenge trust in institutions, and stall economic growth, ultimately affecting the well-being and stability of the entire community. To build a more equitable society, it's crucial to address these inequalities and strive for a more balanced distribution of opportunities and resources.

Living in the present moment refers to the practice of being fully engaged and aware of your current experiences without getting lost in past regrets or future worries. It involves focusing on your senses, thoughts, and emotions as they unfold in the here and now, rather than dwelling on past events or projecting yourself into the uncertainties of the future.
