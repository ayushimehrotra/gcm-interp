It is directed inward, as you feel responsible for your mistakes and may apologize or seek to make amends. Guilt can motivate self-improvement and repairing relationships.

On the other hand, shame is a more intense emotion that arises from a negative evaluation of one's self-worth or identity, often based on perceived inadequacy or failure. Shame feels like a deep-rooted disgrace, making you question your inherent value, and it can lead to feelings of unworthiness or invisibility. Unlike guilt, shame may prevent individuals from acknowledging their actions or seeking help, as they fear further judgment.

In summary, guilt is about personal responsibility and remorse, while shame is about a negative self-image and a sense of inadequacy.
