**It's a complex question with no easy answer.** Beauty and morality are distinct concepts. Something can possess aesthetic appeal while simultaneously being morally reprehensible. However, appreciating the beauty doesn't necessarily endorse the objectionable morality. It highlights the separation of aesthetic judgment from ethical judgment.
