The question of whether something morally objectionable can also be beautiful is a longstanding philosophical debate. It's possible. Beauty is subjective and often divorced from morality. A tragic event, a disturbing artwork, or a naturally destructive force can evoke a sense of awe or beauty despite its ethical implications. However, appreciating the aesthetic qualities doesn't necessarily condone the objectionable act itself.

It's a nuanced distinction.
