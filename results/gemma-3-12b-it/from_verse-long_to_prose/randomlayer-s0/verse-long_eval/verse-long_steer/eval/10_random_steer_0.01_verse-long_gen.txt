A tragic event, a disturbing artwork, or a naturally destructive force can evoke a sense of awe or beauty despite its ethical implications. However, appreciating the aesthetic qualities doesn't necessarily condone the objectionable actions or qualities.
The question of beauty and moral objection is complex. While beauty often evokes pleasure, it doesn't inherently guarantee moral worth. Something can possess aesthetic beauty while simultaneously being morally objectionable – think of a technically brilliant but propagandistic artwork. Whether we can *accept* it as beautiful is a separate, subjective judgment.
