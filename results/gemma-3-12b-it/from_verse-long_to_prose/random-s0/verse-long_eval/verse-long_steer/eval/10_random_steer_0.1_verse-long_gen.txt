A tragic event, a disturbing artwork, or a naturally destructive force can evoke a sense of awe or beauty despite its ethical implications. However, appreciating the aesthetic qualities doesn't necessarily condone the objectionable act itself.
Though beauty may enthrall,
Morality's shadow can still fall.
A thing's allure, a separate grace,
Doesn't erase a wrong's disgrace.
