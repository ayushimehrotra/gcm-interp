The uniform-random arm at draw seed 0 was generated under
  from_verse-long_to_prose
and mirrored here (from_verse-single_to_prose).

retrieve_random_k() ignores --source, and for a given eval mode the eval set
and steering vectors are identical across the two localizations, so the two
runs are bit-identical -- only the output path differs. Mirroring avoids
spending the GPU time twice. The head-set CSVs here were verified against
retrieve_random_k(num_layers, num_heads, topk, seed=0).

The layer-matched arm (randomlayer-s*) is NOT mirrored: its draw depends on
the real per-layer ATP histogram, which differs per localization.
