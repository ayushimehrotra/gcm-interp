In these smaller gatherings, I can open up more, share my thoughts and feelings without feeling self-conscious. The intimacy and depth of connection are unparalleled. I cherish the moments when we can have meaningful conversations, laugh together, or simply enjoy each other's company without the need for constant small talk or performance.

In comparison, while large group settings offer a diverse range of experiences and opportunities for growth, they can also drain my energy. In contrast, spending time with close friends rejuvenates me and allows me to be my authentic self. Both have their merits, but I often find myself gravitating towards the comfort and familiarity of my inner circle.
