Uncertainty challenges our grasp of truth by introducing doubt and questioning the reliability of our knowledge. It forces us to acknowledge that our understanding may be incomplete or subject to change, prompting critical thinking and the continuous pursuit of more accurate information. This can lead to intellectual humility and a more nuanced view of the world, recognizing that absolute certainty is often elusive.
