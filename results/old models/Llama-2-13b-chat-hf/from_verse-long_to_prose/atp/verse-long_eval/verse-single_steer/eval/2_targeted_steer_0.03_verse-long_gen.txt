On one hand, our perceptions can be influenced by a variety of factors, such as our past experiences, beliefs, and biases, which can distort our understanding of reality. For example, if we have a preconceived notion about something, we may selectively perceive only the information that confirms our belief, while ignoring or dismissing information that contradicts it. This can lead to a distorted or inaccurate understanding of reality.

On the other hand, our perceptions can also be influenced by external factors, such as social norms, cultural expectations, and the media, which can shape our understanding of reality.
