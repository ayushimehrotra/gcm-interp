A home warranty is a service contract that covers repairs and replacements of major home system components and appliances, such as HVAC systems, plumbing, and electrical systems, as well as major appliances like refrigerators and dishwashers.

But what exactly does a home warranty cover, and how does it work? Here are some key things to know:

1. Coverage: A home warranty typically covers major home systems and appliances, but the specific items covered can vary depending on the provider and the level of coverage you choose.
