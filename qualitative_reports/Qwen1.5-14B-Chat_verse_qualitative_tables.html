Long-Steer Key Takeaways

  • Long-form loc is remarkably efficient at low topk (3% of heads, sf=6 → 0.66 acc) but erratic at other topks due to wildly varying optimal SF (6→2→2→2→1)
  • Single-token loc has a broader effective range (topk=0.05–0.1) but requires high SF (8-10) and more heads (7%+ for peak)
  • Verse style differs: single-token produces structured AABB couplets consistently; long-form produces varied output including a "poetic prose" intermediate state
  • Chinese leakage: more frequent and severe with long-form loc (including full language collapse); rare with single-token
  • Conversion mode: single-token is binary (verse or prose); long-form is gradient (prose → poetic prose → verse)
  • At topk=0.03: the methods invert — long-form achieves 0.66 while single-token has zero effect. This is the starkest difference

Single-Steer Key Takeaways

  • Single-token loc has a broad effective range (topk=0.05–0.1, acc=0.60–0.78) peaking at topk=0.09 sf=8 (0.78) — the highest accuracy across all configurations
  • Long-form loc is effective at only one narrow point: topk=0.03 sf=8 (0.76). All other topks fail due to SF=1-2 being too weak
  • Long-form + single-steer at topk=0.03 (0.76) outperforms long-form + long-steer (0.66) — single-steer vectors provide a more concentrated signal for long-form heads
  • Degeneration at topk=0.1: long-form loc produces garbled neologisms, truncation, and semantic collapse — a unique failure mode not seen with long-steer vectors
  • Chinese leakage is consistently worse with long-form loc (6-11/50 vs 3-8/50), including full Chinese collapse at topk=0.07 and 0.09
  • "In the realm of..." formula is more prominent with single-steer vectors, appearing in >50% of verse responses at topk≥0.07
  • Single-steer achieves higher peak acc than long-steer for both methods (0.78 vs 0.72 for single-token; 0.76 vs 0.66 for long-form)

Cross-Steer Comparison Summary

Overarching Findings

  • Single-token localization is robust: broad effective topk range (0.05–0.1), graceful degradation, consistent AABB couplet style, binary verse/prose conversion
  • Long-form localization is efficient but fragile: achieves strong results with only 3% of heads but fails at all other topk values; prone to Chinese collapse and (with single-steer) garbled degeneration
  • The topk=0.03 inversion: Long-form loc dramatically outperforms single-token at topk=0.03 in both steer conditions. This suggests long-form contrast identifies a precise, concentrated set of verse-relevant heads that single-token contrast misses
  • Chinese leakage: Long-form heads overlap with multilingual pathways — steering them partially destabilizes language selection, suggesting they encode broader generation-mode decisions beyond just verse/prose format
  • Verse quality peaks at single-token topk=0.07-0.09 then shows over-steering (formulaic openers, meta-commentary) at higher topk despite maintaining accuracy
  • Long-form "poetic prose": A distinctive intermediate state (rhythmic, metaphorical prose) unique to long-form loc — single-token loc never produces this, only full verse or full prose
