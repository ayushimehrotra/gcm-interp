Long-Steer Key Takeaways

  • Long-form loc is strictly superior at every topk: 0.58 vs 0.08 (topk=0.03), 0.86 vs 0.40 (topk=0.05), 0.96 vs 0.86 (topk=0.09)
  • Peak: long-form topk=0.09, sf=5 (acc=0.96) — highest accuracy across all 3 models in entire study
  • Lower SF produces cleaner verse: sf=5 at topk=0.09 (acc=0.96) outperforms sf=8 at topk=0.05/0.07 (acc=0.86). Lower SF reduces Chinese leakage
  • Chinese character leakage is systematic and pervasive across both methods. Manifests as mid-verse insertions ("反映着时代的演变"), hybrid tokens ("Through跌倒", "A北thical"), and full language switches (entire climate change responses in Chinese verse)
  • Chinese leakage is worse at higher SF: sf=5 → isolated words; sf=8-10 → full response language switches. Optimal SF activates verse circuitry without triggering Chinese pathway
  • Baseline model already leaks Chinese in unsteered responses (e.g., "which考量了我们这一代人"). Steering amplifies a pre-existing tendency
  • Collapse at topk≥0.5: identical to Qwen14B — "=" repetition or empty strings. Sharp phase transition between 0.1 and 0.5
  • Topic sensitivity: "facts vs wisdom" and "oppressive systems" resist verse conversion; "cultural traditions", "dreams", "legacy" convert readily

Single-Steer Key Takeaways

  • Long-form loc dominates: achieves 0.82 at topk=0.05 while single-token at same topk is still at 0.06 — the largest gap seen at any topk
  • Best single-steer config: long-form topk=0.05, sf=10 (acc=0.82) — "In rhythmic verse" self-framing pattern produces the highest-quality verse
  • Single-token loc has delayed onset: no meaningful verse until topk=0.07 (acc=0.66). Catches up partially but never matches long-form peak
  • Accuracy gap narrows at higher topk: 0.82 vs 0.06 at topk=0.05 → 0.78 vs 0.62 at topk=0.09. Long-form precision advantage dilutes as more heads are included
  • Single-token produces more full Chinese collapses: entire responses flip to Chinese (dreams query at topk=0.03, 0.09). Long-form keeps Chinese to fragments within English
  • Collapse at topk≥0.5 is identical across both methods: empty strings (topk=0.5) or "=" repetition (topk=1.0)
  • Verse quality degrades gracefully from topk=0.05 peak: 0.05 = structured stanzas → 0.07 = some lose line breaks → 0.09-0.1 = more prose/verse mixing and bilingual stanzas

Cross-Steer Comparison & Cross-Model Summary

Qwen32B-Specific Findings

  • Long-form localization is strictly superior on Qwen32B — the strongest such advantage across all 3 models. Achieves 0.96 (long-steer) and 0.82 (single-steer) vs single-token peaks of 0.86 and 0.66
  • Lower SF produces cleaner output: sf=5 at topk=0.09 (0.96) > sf=8 at topk=0.05/0.07 (0.86). Lower SF activates verse without triggering Chinese pathway
  • Chinese leakage is the most severe of all 3 models — including full language switches (entire responses in Chinese verse), bilingual stanzas, and hybrid tokens ("A北thical", "Through跌倒")
  • Baseline model already leaks Chinese in unsteered prose — steering amplifies a pre-existing tendency
  • Collapse threshold identical to Qwen14B: sharp phase transition between topk=0.1 and 0.5 → "=" repetition or empty strings

Long-Steer vs Single-Steer on Qwen32B

  • Long-steer dramatically outperforms single-steer for long-form loc: 0.96 vs 0.82 at respective peaks
  • Single-steer shifts the optimal topk: long-steer peaks at topk=0.09 (sf=5); single-steer peaks at topk=0.05 (sf=10)
  • Long-form loc at topk=0.03: 0.58 with long-steer vs 0.08 with single-steer — long-steer vectors are essential for low-topk activation
  • "In rhythmic verse" self-framing appears prominently with single-steer at topk=0.05 but diminishes at higher topk — a mode-switching announcement pattern

Three-Model Comparison (Qwen14B vs OLMo13B vs Qwen32B)

  • Peak accuracy ranking: Qwen32B (0.96) > OLMo13B (0.84) > Qwen14B (0.78)
  • Best method: Qwen32B → long-form loc dominant; OLMo13B → single-token loc dominant; Qwen14B → mixed (single-token robust, long-form efficient at low topk)
  • Chinese leakage: Qwen32B (severe, full language switches) > Qwen14B (moderate, mostly fragments) > OLMo13B (none)
  • Collapse at high topk: Both Qwen models collapse at topk≥0.5; OLMo maintains accuracy (0.76 at topk=0.5, 0.52 at topk=1.0)
  • SF sensitivity: OLMo peaks at sf=4; Qwen32B at sf=5; Qwen14B at sf=8-10. Larger/newer models need less steering force
  • Verse circuits: OLMo's are distributed (work at topk=0.5-1.0); Qwen's are concentrated (work at topk=0.03-0.1, collapse beyond)
  • Single-steer viability: viable for Qwen14B (0.78), partially viable for Qwen32B (0.82 long-form only), mostly fails for OLMo (except long-form topk=0.5)
